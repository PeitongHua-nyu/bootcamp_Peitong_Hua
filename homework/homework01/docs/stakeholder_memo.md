\# Stakeholder Memo



\## Stakeholder

Incoming NYU Tandon students searching for off-campus housing in Downtown Brooklyn.



\## Decision

Students need to decide which Downtown Brooklyn apartment option best fits their budget, desired proximity to campus, and preferred building features.



\## Key Needs

Students need clear comparisons of monthly rent, distance to NYU Tandon, apartment type, and building amenities.



\## Project Goal

The project will organize and analyze Downtown Brooklyn apartment information so that incoming students can compare housing options more efficiently and make a more informed rental decision.



\## Risks

Rental prices and availability change frequently, and some buildings may have incomplete or inconsistent public information.
